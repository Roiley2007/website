# The Chronicle of Grasshollow

Everything the village noticed, in the order it happened.

## Day 1

- **16:00** — The weather turned to rain.

## Day 2

- **11:50** — The weather turned to clear.

## Day 3

- **05:35** — The weather turned to storm.
- **05:55** — Tam Coble prayed for a bridge over the ford.
- **14:05** — Bram Halloway prayed for word from his daughter Wren.
- **18:35** — The weather turned to cloudy.

## Day 4

- **06:05** — Tam Coble prayed for someone to be near.
- **06:10** — Odile Fenn prayed to know where she came from.
- **06:25** — The weather turned to clear.
- **06:40** — Odile Fenn prayed for books, and someone who can read them.
- **10:50** — Bram Halloway prayed for a plough that holds together.
- **13:30** — The weather turned to rain.

## Day 5

- **07:40** — The weather turned to storm.
- **14:20** — The weather turned to clear.

## Day 6

- **03:25** — The weather turned to rain.
